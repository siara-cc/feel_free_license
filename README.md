# The Feel Free License

> **Feel free to develop. Feel free to share. Let users feel free to pay back the value they received.**

The Feel Free License is a **user-friendly and developer-friendly license** that combines the following principles:

* **Feel free to create:** Develop and create anything, from ideas and designs to source code, art, documents, and other creative works.
* **Feel free to share:** Share the Work freely, without restrictions on use, distribution, modification, or commercial use.
* **Feel free to give back:** Let users assess the value they have received and, entirely at their own discretion, pay a proportionate license fee to the author.

Freedom to use is unconditional, while compensation is entirely voluntary.

## Why The Feel Free License?

Open licenses have traditionally ranged from **restrictive or reciprocal licenses**, such as GPL and LGPL, to **permissive licenses**, such as the MIT License and Apache License.

These licenses primarily define the freedoms, permissions, and obligations of the people who use the work. They generally do not provide a mechanism within the license itself for users to voluntarily recognize or compensate the people who created and shared the work.

As a result, the decision to freely share valuable work is often driven entirely by the author's desire to contribute to the community. While this can make useful work widely available, it may not always provide an incentive or opportunity for authors to sustain, improve, or continue creating that work.

The Feel Free License hopes to complement the philosophy of permissive licensing by explicitly recognizing both sides:

* **Freedom for users to use and build upon the Work without obligation**, and
* **Freedom for users to voluntarily return value to the author when they believe they have received value from the Work.**

The purpose is not to require payment or restrict access. Rather, it is to encourage a voluntary relationship in which authors can share freely and users can freely choose to give back.

In this way, the Feel Free License aims to make sharing work freely not only beneficial to users, but also more sustainable and rewarding for the people who create it.

## A permissive license for any kind of creative or intellectual work

The **Feel Free License** is a simple, non-restrictive license intended for works of any kind.

It is inspired by permissive licenses such as the MIT and BSD licenses, but is not limited to software or source code. It may be used for software, documentation, designs, artistic works, written works, technical works, creative works, intellectual works, specifications, and other materials to the extent that the author has rights to license them.

The basic principle is simple:

> **You are free to use the work. If it gives you value and you wish to support the author, you are free to compensate them for that value—but you are never required to do so.**

## Voluntary license fee

The Feel Free License permits, but does not require, a user of the Work to pay the author a **voluntary license fee or contribution**.

The amount may be determined entirely at the user's discretion and may reflect the value that the user believes they have received from the Work.

Such a payment is intended to:

* encourage the author to continue creating, maintaining, or making works available to the public;
* recognize or compensate the author for the value voluntarily provided through the Work; and
* support the continued availability or development of similar works.

### There is absolutely no obligation to pay

Payment of a voluntary license fee is **not a condition of using, copying, modifying, distributing, or otherwise exercising rights granted under this License**.

The Work may be used without payment.

A user may decide to pay nothing.

A user may pay any amount they consider appropriate.

Failure or refusal to pay creates no breach of this License.

## Intended spirit

The Feel Free License is based on the idea that making a work freely available does not mean that the work has no value.

The author gives you broad freedom to use the Work.

If the Work provides meaningful value to you and you wish to recognize or support the author, you may voluntarily do so.

In short:

> **Feel free to use it. Feel free to build on it. Feel free to share it. And, if you feel it has given you value, feel free to give something back.**
